Job Title: Maintenance Technician I-Conversion

Shift: Monday-Friday 8:00AM-5:00PM

Department: Top Cap

Job Summary:

The Maintenance Technician - Conversion - modifies production machinery for new cell models and maintains the production machinery in proper operating order through mechanical and electrical hardware changes and has demonstrated knowledge of up to 10% of SOPs within a specific manufacturing process. This role will be exposed to troubleshooting, repair, and improvement activities. Will also be required to perform the functions of an operator.

Essential Duties:

  • Perform mechanical and electrical hardware changes to accommodate new cell model requirements
  • Measure and adjust cell dimensions to meet cell specifications
  • Inspect, organize, and prepare incoming hardware shipments for upcoming line conversions
  • Check settings, gauge readings, and sensors regularly to keep machines operating at proper specifications
  • Perform regular preventative maintenance on machines according to documented requirements
  • Make continuing visual and audible observations of the machines to detect any abnormalities
  • Tighten bolts and inspects belts and chains for wear and tear for proper tensions
  • Perform simple machinist duties and responsibilities such as periodic cleaning and lubrication of machines, change oil and swap out parts
  • Restore machines to correct operating functions
  • Make the needed mechanical and pneumatic adjustments
  • Ability to recover all error codes
  • Use a variety of hand and power tools, electric meters, and material handling equipment in performing duties
  • Report faulty operations and defective material to proper supervision
  • Support modifications to the machines and overall process to help increase the efficiency and production of the department
  • Partner with operators on how to complete cleaning tasks, minor repairs and adjustments to the machines
  • Maintain complete and accurate records of all work done on the equipment and machines

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements:

  • To ensure the health and safety in the workplace and for the protection of our employees, wearing PPE is a possibility and may include equipment such as a full Tyvek suit, safety shoes, gloves, safety glasses, face mask, bump cap, and a full hazmat suit that includes a respirator.

About Us:

Panasonic Energy Corporation of North America (PECNA) is collaborating with Tesla at a massive battery factory near Reno, Nevada which is known for its quality of life and expansive outdoor adventures. Panasonic's role at the Gigafactory is to manufacture and supply cylindrical lithium-ion cells to Tesla for their electric vehicles and energy storage systems.

Over the past few years, the Gigafactory has grown to the largest lithium-ion battery factory in the world. Together with our partner Tesla, we continue to revolutionize the lithium-ion battery space and accelerate the world’s transition to sustainable energy and transportation.

Our mission at PECNA is to make the vision of affordable Electric Vehicles a reality by producing the world’s safest, high quality, and lowest cost batteries. Through this effort we will create a clean energy society and our products will change society’s use of and perceptions of electric power.

In addition to an environment that is as innovative as our products, we offer competitive salaries and benefits.
