Controls Engineer II

📍 Lake Oswego, OR

Job Description

- This role does require heavy travel to the client site

- Looking for candidate who reside in the Portland OR and Boise ID locations.

Who You'll Work With

- At Design Group, you'll collaborate with experienced leaders, technical specialists, and subject matter experts to solve complex challenges for some of the world's leading companies.

- You'll join a growing team of more than 1,500 professionals across 45 U.S. offices where you'll be empowered to make an impact, develop your expertise, and grow your career.

What You'll Do

- In this role, you'll be responsible for developing and implementing automation solutions that support capital projects and manufacturing operations.

- You'll work closely with clients, vendors, project stakeholders, and Control System Integration Practice teams to design, configure, test, commission, and start up integrated control systems.

- This position plays an important role in helping clients improve performance and deliver automated manufacturing processes, giving you the opportunity to apply technical expertise across PLC, HMI/SCADA, control panel, and commissioning activities.

- Develop automation solutions through design specifications, software configuration, testing, commissioning, and start-up activities

- Design automation control systems and create project documentation and drawings that meet client requirements

- Program PLCs and HMI/SCADA systems, including Allen-Bradley, Rockwell, FactoryTalk, Cimplicity, Wonderware, and Ignition platforms

- Configure software and hardware packages according to client design specifications

- Diagnose automation problems and support troubleshooting in high-intensity manufacturing environments

- Communicate project progress, risks, and technical updates to stakeholders throughout the engagement

- Manage engineering project deliverables to agreed schedule, scope, and client expectations

- Coordinate site construction, installation, and start-up support for integrated control systems

- Validate and commission facility systems using established execution protocols

- Support successful system turnover to customers through clear documentation, testing, and technical communication

What You'll Bring

- Qualifications

- 3+ years of direct controls engineering experience with automated processes or system integration in food, beverage, consumer health product, or other industrial manufacturing environments

- Experience creating and designing automation control systems, including PLC and SCADA systems

- Working knowledge of industrial control panel design and fabrication, instrumentation and controls, and engineering documentation such as P&IDs and process flow diagrams

- Experience with field instrumentation, control system installation, and configuration

- Ability to travel for project requirements, including installation and start-up activities, client and company meetings, trainings, seminars, forums, and conventions

Preferred Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in electrical, mechanical, chemical engineering, or a similar technical field

- Familiarity with machine safety applications, electrical power distribution, and control panel design

- Experience generating reports and conducting group presentations for technical audiences

The approximate pay range for this position is $115k-$150k
