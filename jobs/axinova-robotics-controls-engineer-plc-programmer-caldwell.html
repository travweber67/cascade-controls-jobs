AXINOVA ROBOTICS: Controls Engineer / PLC Programmer - Caldwell, ID

Employer: Axinova Robotics

Job Description:

Controls Engineer / PLC Programmer

Axinova Inc. – Caldwell, ID

$65,000–$85,000/year | Full-time | In-person

Compensation and title based on experience, education, and technical competencies.

Build Custom Automation Systems

Axinova is a robotic systems integrator specializing in custom automation solutions for the food and beverage industry. We design, build, program, and commission advanced secondary packaging systems including robotic case packing, palletizing, material handling, conveying, and other custom automation solutions for industrial customers across the United States.

We are looking for a Junior Controls Engineer / PLC Programmer who is interested in industrial automation, robotics, and machine controls and wants to build a career working on real-world automation systems.

This is a hands-on engineering position where you will work alongside experienced controls, robotics, electrical, and mechanical engineers to develop, test, troubleshoot, and commission custom automation equipment.

Our systems primarily use Allen-Bradley / Rockwell Automation PLC and HMI platforms and commonly integrate KUKA and ABB industrial robots, servo systems, VFDs, safety devices, vision systems, and other industrial automation equipment.

Previous industrial automation experience is helpful, but we are also interested in recent engineering graduates or candidates with a strong technical foundation who are motivated to learn.

Responsibilities

Develop and modify Allen-Bradley PLC programs using Studio 5000

Assist with programming ControlLogix and CompactLogix PLC systems

Develop and modify HMI applications using Rockwell FactoryTalk platforms

Assist with machine sequencing, interlocks, alarms, and fault handling

Learn and work within Axinova's standardized PLC and HMI programming practices

Assist with integration of KUKA, ABB, and other industrial robotic systems

Configure and troubleshoot industrial devices connected through EtherNet/IP

Work with VFDs, servo drives, remote I/O, sensors, safety devices, barcode systems, vision systems, and other automation equipment

Assist with machine safety systems and safety PLC applications

Support robotic case packing, palletizing, conveying, material handling, and secondary packaging systems

Perform controls checkout, I/O testing, machine startup, and troubleshooting

Assist with Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), Site Acceptance Testing (SAT), installation, and commissioning

Troubleshoot electrical, controls, networking, and equipment integration issues

Work closely with mechanical, electrical, robotics, fabrication, and field service teams

Assist with customer startups and commissioning projects

Create and maintain PLC/HMI backups, documentation, and troubleshooting information

Learn Axinova's PackML-based machine architecture, reusable programming structures, naming conventions, and HMI standards

Required Qualifications

Degree, technical training, or equivalent experience in electrical engineering, electrical engineering technology, mechatronics, automation, robotics, computer engineering, or a related technical field

Basic understanding of PLC programming and industrial automation

Familiarity with ladder logic and PLC programming concepts

Basic understanding of electrical controls, sensors, motors, and industrial equipment

Ability to read and understand electrical schematics and technical documentation

Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills

Comfortable working with computers, networking, and industrial software

Willingness to learn new automation technologies and programming standards

Ability to work effectively as part of a multidisciplinary engineering team

Strong communication and organizational skills

Willingness to travel for installation, startup, and commissioning projects

Preferred Qualifications

These qualifications are helpful but not required:

Internship, co-op, school, or work experience involving PLC programming or industrial automation

Experience with Allen-Bradley PLCs or Studio 5000

Familiarity with ControlLogix or CompactLogix platforms

Experience with Rockwell FactoryTalk View

Familiarity with industrial robots such as KUKA or ABB

Experience with VFDs, servo motors, sensors, or industrial control systems

Familiarity with EtherNet/IP or other industrial communication networks

Experience building, wiring, programming, or troubleshooting automated equipment

Exposure to machine safety systems or safety PLCs

Experience with robotics, mechatronics, manufacturing, or automated machinery

Familiarity with structured PLC programming, AOIs, UDTs, or state-machine concepts

Previous experience in food, beverage, packaging, manufacturing, or material handling environments

Travel Expectations

This position includes travel for system installation, startup, troubleshooting, and commissioning.

Typical travel ranges from approximately 20–40% depending on project phase. Some periods may require increased travel during active customer deployments.

Travel pay

Why Join Axinova?

Build real industrial automation systems rather than working only in simulation or theory

Gain hands-on experience with Allen-Bradley PLCs, industrial robots, servo systems, VFDs, and machine controls

Work on projects from initial assembly and programming through final customer commissioning

Learn from experienced controls, robotics, electrical, and mechanical engineers

Work with KUKA and ABB industrial robotic systems

Develop PLC, HMI, troubleshooting, and system integration skills

Gain field experience installing and commissioning automated equipment

Work on a wide variety of custom automation projects

Opportunity to quickly take on additional responsibility as your technical skills develop

Grow your career with an expanding automation integrator
