Fulcrum Technologies Inc

Industrial Maintenance Technician

📍 Tualatin, OR

💲 Published pay: $30–$35

Position Overview

The Industrial Maintenance Technician is responsible for the maintenance, repair, and upkeep of facility infrastructure, building systems, and manufacturing equipment throughout our operation.

In addition to facility maintenance responsibilities, this role supports routine maintenance, troubleshooting, and repair of manufacturing equipment and related systems. The position plays a critical role in minimizing downtime, supporting production, and maintaining a safe and efficient workplace.

The ideal candidate is mechanically inclined, resourceful, dependable, and capable of independently diagnosing and resolving a variety of maintenance issues in a manufacturing environment.

This position description is intended to describe the general nature and level of work performed by employees in this role. Duties, responsibilities, and qualifications may be modified as business needs evolve.

What You'll Do

Facility Maintenance & Repairs

Perform routine facility maintenance, repair, and general upkeep throughout the building and surrounding property.

Maintain building infrastructure including lighting, doors, windows, locks, plumbing fixtures, and related systems.

Troubleshoot facility-related issues and perform corrective repairs.

Respond to maintenance requests from employees and departments.

Inspect facility systems and identify maintenance concerns before they become larger problems.

Coordinate with outside contractors and service providers when required.

Maintain safe, clean, and organized work areas.

Preventive Maintenance

Perform scheduled preventive maintenance on facility systems and manufacturing equipment.

Conduct inspections to identify wear, leaks, mechanical concerns, and potential failures.

Maintain maintenance records and documentation.

Assist with improving preventive maintenance programs and procedures.

Identify recurring equipment issues and recommend practical solutions to improve reliability and reduce costs.

Monitor facility and equipment conditions and communicate concerns requiring corrective action.

Manufacturing Equipment Support

Support routine maintenance and troubleshooting of manufacturing equipment.

Perform qualified in-house repairs to reduce equipment downtime.

Conduct manufacturer-recommended maintenance activities, including cleaning, lubrication, adjustments, and component replacement.

Support maintenance activities involving:

Flex Gantry Mill

Flex WaterJet

Doosan CNC Machinery

Welding Equipment

Sandblasting Equipment

Industrial Air Compressors and Compressed Air Systems

Identify signs of wear, damage, leaks, and equipment malfunctions.

Determine when specialized support or outside service providers are required.

Safety & Compliance

Follow all company safety policies and procedures.

Perform work in accordance with Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) requirements.

Utilize required PPE and safe work practices.

Identify and report unsafe conditions and equipment hazards.

Support facility improvement projects that enhance workplace safety and operational efficiency.

Perform only work for which appropriate training and authorization have been obtained.

Maintenance Coordination

Maintain tools, equipment, and maintenance supplies in proper working condition.

Monitor inventory levels of commonly used maintenance parts and supplies.

Coordinate maintenance activities with production personnel, contractors, and vendors.

Support facility improvement projects, equipment installations, and operational upgrades.

Recommend practical improvements that increase reliability and reduce maintenance costs.

What We're Looking For

Required Qualifications

Minimum of three (3) years of industrial or manufacturing maintenance experience in a hands-on maintenance role.

Experience working in a manufacturing or industrial environment.

Strong mechanical aptitude and troubleshooting skills.

Experience performing preventive maintenance programs.

Ability to troubleshoot and repair mechanical equipment with minimal supervision.

Familiarity with pneumatic and hydraulic systems.

Experience troubleshooting motors, pumps, bearings, belts, gearboxes, valves, and other mechanical components.

Working knowledge of common hand tools, power tools, and maintenance equipment.

Ability to read and interpret equipment manuals, maintenance procedures, parts diagrams, and technical documentation.

Strong organizational, communication, and time-management skills.

Ability to prioritize maintenance requests based on urgency and operational impact.

Ability to work safely in a manufacturing environment.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience maintaining CNC machining equipment.

Experience supporting waterjet systems, gantry mills, or similar heavy manufacturing equipment.

Welding and/or fabrication experience.

Experience working with industrial compressed-air systems.

Basic electrical troubleshooting experience.

Experience managing preventive maintenance software or maintenance tracking systems.

Experience coordinating contractors, equipment service providers, and vendors.

Performance Expectations

Successful candidates will demonstrate the ability to:

Maintain facility infrastructure in a safe and functional condition.

Complete maintenance requests in a timely and professional manner.

Perform preventive maintenance consistently and accurately.

Identify potential equipment and facility issues before they result in downtime.

Resolve routine maintenance issues while exercising sound judgment regarding when specialized support is required.

Reduce avoidable downtime and unnecessary service expenditures.

Communicate effectively with supervisors, production personnel, and outside vendors.

Contribute to a safe, organized, and efficient manufacturing environment.

Work Environment

Active manufacturing environment with exposure to industrial equipment and machinery.

Frequent standing, walking, bending, kneeling, reaching, lifting, and carrying throughout the workday.

Work performed both indoors and outdoors as facility needs require.

Use of ladders and elevated work areas when authorized.

Required use of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), including safety glasses, hearing protection, and safety footwear.

Schedule

This position will initially train on Day Shift to learn facility operations, maintenance procedures, equipment, and safety practices.

Following training, the position is expected to transition to either a Swing Shift or Weekend Shift based on business needs. Candidates must be flexible and willing to work either schedule.

Additional overtime or schedule adjustments may occasionally be required.
