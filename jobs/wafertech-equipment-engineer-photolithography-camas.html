WaferTech

Equipment Engineer - Photolithography

📍 Camas, WA

WaferTech currently has an Equipment Engineer for the Photolithography Department. We are seeking a driven and goal oriented individual to work in a fast-paced semiconductor manufacturing environment. The role of equipment Engineer has a number of challenges and will require exceptional teamwork, communication skills and demonstrated strength in our core values of Integrity, Customer Partnership, Innovation, and commitment. The position is an exempt position with primary M-F work hours.

  • Perform data analysis using SPC, Real-time monitoring methods, and additional sources to monitor and maintain the performance of the equipment.
  • Plan and perform preventive maintenance of equipment with the technician team.
  • Develop and implement improvements for Availability and Reliability equipment.
  • Continuous Improvement in Quality performance, cost and Throughput.
  • Track and maintain adequate spare parts and inventory for maintenance and needs to limit tool down time.
  • Interact with both internal customers and vendors as needed to improve the performance and the modification of equipment as needed.
  • Supervise and support the installation or move equipment.
  • Troubleshoot tool issues of complex electric and mechanical systems to quickly diagnose and repair.

WaferTech is an integrated circuit (IC) semiconductor foundry in the United States. Our customers (other businesses) partner with WaferTech to manufacture the computer chips that the client has designed. The chips that we make end up in a wide variety of products that the average consumer relies upon in their daily lives. Cell phones, pacemakers, cars, and security are some examples of how our semiconductors are used.

Located in Camas, Washington, a suburb of Portland, Oregon, we are backed by the technological prowess of our parent company, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC), creator and world leader in the semiconductor integrated circuit foundry industry.

While interviews may have a virtual component due to pandemic precautions, all WaferTech positions are conducted on-site and remote work is not offered.

Visa sponsorship is not available for this position now or in the future.

We thank you for considering employment with WaferTech.
