WSDOT: Electrical and Electronic Systems Specialist 3 | Poulsbo, Kitsap County, WA

Employer: Washington State Department of Transportation

Job Description:

About WSDOT

The Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT) is a multimodal agency with a global reputation for excellence. Our dedicated workforce plans, designs, builds, and operates an integrated transportation system that safely and efficiently moves people and goods throughout the state. In addition to maintaining over 20,000 lane miles of state highway and 4,100 bridges, WSDOT leads an award-winning Active Transportation Plan, manages the world's longest floating bridge, and operates the largest ferry system in the nation!

The Opportunity

WSDOT is currently seeking an Electrical and Electronic Systems Specialist 3 to serve as the senior technical expert and lead for the Hood Canal Bridge maintenance organization. In this role, you will help ensure the bridge’s critical electrical, electronic, monitoring, and control systems operate safely and reliably. Your expertise and leadership will support effective maintenance and repair, helping protect the traveling public and WSDOT employees while keeping this vital transportation connection operating. Join WSDOT in supporting safe, reliable transportation and the long-term resilience of Washington’s transportation infrastructure.

What to Expect

This position involves physically demanding bridge electrical and maintenance work performed outdoors year-round in all weather conditions, during day and night shifts, and near high-speed traffic and bodies of water. Work may occur in confined spaces and at heights up to 200 feet, requiring climbing, maneuvering to 50 pounds, and using fall protection, rigging equipment, and specialized tools. Employees may be exposed to hazardous materials, extreme weather, loud noise, and other highway maintenance hazards. The position typically works four 10-hour shifts, with extended hours, nights, weekends, holidays, standby, and emergency callbacks as needed.

Among the varied range of responsibilities held within this role, you will:

Perform, lead, and direct preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, testing, installation, and repair of Hood Canal Bridge electrical, electronic, monitoring, communication, power generation, and control systems; mentor and instruct junior staff in safe and efficient work practices.

Inspect and test electrical voltages, connections, and grounding systems; identify, document, prioritize, and complete corrective maintenance to ensure system safety, reliability, and integrity.

Ensure compliance with WSDOT Standards and Specifications, the National Electrical Code (NEC), Washington Administrative Code (WAC) electrical standards, the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD), and applicable safety procedures, including electrical hazard awareness, arc flash protection, job hazard analysis, and Pre-Activity Safety Plans (PASP).

Plan, schedule, and coordinate daily through annual preventive maintenance and repair activities, including work methods, equipment, materials, and electrical testing procedures, using computerized maintenance management systems.

Maintain maintenance records, asset inventories, inspection reports, test results, work reports, timesheets, preventive maintenance documentation, and safety records in accordance with departmental and state and federal requirements; procure and maintain necessary tools, materials, and repair parts.

Conduct periodic, post-earthquake, and event-driven bridge inspections; document inspection activities in designated tracking systems and provide inspection data to Bridge Preservation Office (BPO) engineering staff while coordinating repairs.

Respond to bridge strikes, accidents, spills, severe weather, and other emergencies; establish and operate traffic control for lane closures, detours, and bridge or road closures in accordance with departmental standards and MUTCD requirements, and coordinate responses with internal staff and external agencies.

Support storm response and area maintenance operations, including snow and ice control; promote workplace and public safety through required training, safety meetings, proper use and maintenance of PPE and fall protection equipment, safe hazardous-material handling, hazard identification, and reporting of injuries and accidents.

Qualifications

To be considered for this opportunity, the following competencies are required:

To qualify at the EESS Trainee level, the following is required:

Introductory Electrical and Electronic Systems Skills: Basic understanding of and/or aptitude for technical electrical and/or electronic work.

To qualify at the EESS1 level, the following are required:

Entry Level Electrical and Electronic Systems Skills: Demonstrated ability to perform sub-journey technical electrical and/or electronic work. Introductory knowledge of relevant codes, regulations, and industry standards.

Safety and Technical Knowledge: Entry-level knowledge and understanding of programmable logic controllers, motor controllers, cathodic protection systems, high-inertial machinery and braking components.

To qualify at the EESS2 level, the following are required:

Journey Level Electrical and Electronic Systems Skills: Demonstrated ability to perform journey-level technical electrical and/or electronic work. Demonstrated knowledge of relevant codes, regulations, and industry standards.

Safety and Technical Knowledge: Journey-level knowledge and understanding of programmable logic controllers, motor controllers, cathodic protection systems, high-inertial machinery and braking components.

Licensing & Certification: Approximately 8,000 hours of relevant professional experience performing technical electrical and/or electronic work

To qualify at the EESS3 level, the following are required:

Senior/Specialist Electrical and Electronic Systems Skills: Demonstrated ability to perform senior level work with technical electrical and/or electronic work. Proven and demonstrate knowledge of relevant codes, regulations, and industry standards. Ability to assess PM needs and perform repairs.

Safety and Technical Knowledge: Demonstrated knowledge and understanding of programmable logic controllers, motor controllers, cathodic protection systems, high-inertial machinery and braking components.

Leadership: Demonstrated skills to lead EESS in-training employees. Establishes clear expectations, ensures compliance with safety and operational standards, and promotes accountability, teamwork, and employee development through effective communication and coaching.

Licensing & Certification: Approximately 10,000 hours of relevant professional experience performing technical electrical and/or electronic work.

The following qualifications are also required:

Collaborative Partnership & Customer Focus: Proven ability to build, cultivate, and sustain productive working relationships with internal and external stakeholders to advance agency goals and deliver effective service outcomes.

Safety & Technical Knowledge: Demonstrated knowledge of workplace safety standards, electrical and electronic systems work, construction industry practices, and heavy equipment operations applicable to bridge and highway maintenance and transportation infrastructure work. Demonstrated knowledge of structural systems, including concrete, steel, and wood. Demonstrated knowledge of fall-protection PPE and rigging gear; proven safety record working at heights and/or similarly high-risk environments.

Communication: Strong written, verbal, and presentation skills with the ability to convey technical and non-technical information clearly and professionally to diverse audiences, ensuring mutual understanding and respect.

Conflict Resolution: Ability to address and manage confrontational situations with composure, ensuring workplace challenges are resolved professionally and respectfully. Proficiency in de-escalation strategies to diffuse tense interactions.

Technical & Digital Proficiency: Proficiency in operating Windows-based systems and business software, including Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, Word, Outlook, SharePoint), cloud-based platforms, virtual meeting tools (e.g., Microsoft Teams), and agency databases used for project tracking, asset management, and information systems.

Licensing & Certification:

Possession of an unrestricted Class B CDL with N endorsement to legally operate vehicles in Washington State within six (6) months of hire.

The ability to obtain a valid Traffic Control/Flagging card within six (6) months of hire.

The ability to obtain a valid First Aid/CPR Certification within six (6) months of hire.

Growth Mindset: Actively demonstrates a commitment to learning and growth.

Service-Oriented: Takes action to meet the needs of others.

It is preferred that qualified candidates also demonstrate:

Electrical/Electronic Licenses:

Journey Level Electrician (ME01, EL01).

Electronics Certification by Washington State CET or International Society of Certified Electronics Technicians (ISCET).

Other electrical/electronic systems education.
