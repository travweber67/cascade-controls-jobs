Maintenance Technician (E&I)

Job ID: 41964 | Location: Clear Lake, Texas, USA | Work Model: Onsite

Join Clariant as an Industrial E&I Maintenance Technician!

In this role, you will help keep our production operations running safely and reliably by installing, calibrating, troubleshooting, and maintaining electrical and instrumentation equipment in a chemical manufacturing environment. You will interpret technical drawings, maintain measurement devices, valves, and actuators, and ensure electrical systems meet safety standards. You will also document maintenance activities and collaborate with the team to support reliable plant operations.

Become part of a great team where you can apply your experience, develop your skills, and continue growing your career!

Responsibilities

  • Interpret and work from complex instrumentation drawings, including P&IDs, loop sheets, and isometric diagrams
  • Install calibrate, and maintain a wide range of measurement devices for flow, pressure, level and temperature
  • Perform advanced electrical testing and troubleshooting using specialized equipment like data recording devices and field communicators
  • Work with hazardous location equipment, ensuring proper installation and maintenance according to safety standards
  • Inspect and maintain grounding systems
  • Perform advanced conduit and tubing installation, including complex bends and layouts
  • Trouble shoot and maintain valves and actuators, including electrical, pneumatic, and hydraulic systems
  • Record all maintenance activities in SAP

Requirements

  • High school diploma, NCCER certifications
  • 5+ year in a chemical manufacturing environment performing equipment maintenance
  • Exposure to SAP
  • NCCER Level three Industrial Electrical and instrumentation Maintenance Technician Assessment
