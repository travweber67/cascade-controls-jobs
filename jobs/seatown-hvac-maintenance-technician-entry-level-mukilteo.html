SEATOWN: HVAC Maintenance Technician | Entry Level - Mukilteo, WA

Employer: Seatown Electric Plumbing Heating and Air

Job Description:

Ready to put your HVAC skills to work and keep building your career? Join our team as an HVAC Maintenance Technician, installing, maintaining, and repairing systems in residential and commercial properties.

You'll use advanced diagnostic tools to troubleshoot problems, perform routine and preventive maintenance, and help keep systems running efficiently. Along the way, you'll answer customer questions, address concerns professionally, follow safety standards and regulations, and keep accurate records of services performed and parts used.

What We Offer

Health Coverage: Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance.

Retirement Savings: A 401(k) plan with company match.

Paid Time Off: PTO, holidays, and sick leave.

Onsite Gym: Access to an onsite gym.

Training and Development: Ongoing opportunities to build your skills and advance your career.

Supportive Team Environment: An inclusive workplace focused on teamwork, quality service, and employee satisfaction.

Who We're Looking For

We're hiring 4 HVAC Maintenance Technicians to join our team!

You're an experienced HVAC technician who takes pride in your work and delivers professional, courteous customer service. You'll bring:

A minimum of 2 years of experience as an HVAC technician.

A high school diploma or GED and completion of a relevant HVAC training program or apprenticeship.

A valid HVAC technician license and driver's license.

Strong technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to diagnose and resolve HVAC issues.

Attention to detail, effective communication skills, and a commitment to safe work practices.

Physical Requirements

Ability to work in varied conditions, including confined spaces and at heights, with the physical stamina and dexterity needed for HVAC installation, maintenance, and repair work.

Ready to take the next step in your HVAC career?

Bring your skills to a team that values customer service and supports your professional growth. Apply today!

Pay Range

$22 - $28 USD
